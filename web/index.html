Upload a question paper

You crop each question, diagram or passage yourself after upload. The app reads exactly what is inside your box: text, formulas and the drawings in it.

⚡ Every box is read by Mathpix (online): about 1–3 s per question, ≈ ₹0.17 each. Only the boxes you draw are sent, never the whole page.

A PDF or the original image works best. Screenshots and compressed photos lose detail.
